# User guide — salespeople and sales managers

All dates and times are shown in Dubai time. Your login is personal; never share it. Sign out on shared devices.

## Signing in
Open the platform URL, enter your work e-mail and password. If you were given a temporary password you will be asked to set a new one (at least 10 characters with upper-case, lower-case and a number).

## My Dashboard
Your personal KPIs: total, new, quotations sent, negotiations, pending client responses, confirmed, lost, overdue follow-ups, estimated pipeline revenue and confirmed revenue — covering **only enquiries assigned to you**. The follow-up list shows what is due in the next seven days; red means overdue.

## Registering an enquiry (New Enquiry)
1. **Enquiry information** — property, source, type and priority. The Enquiry ID (e.g. `FH-FCH-2026-0042`), creation time and your name are set automatically.
2. **Client / company** — company and contact are mandatory.
3. **Accommodation** — arrival and departure (nights are calculated), rooms, category, guests, meal plan. Stays of 28+ nights are classed as Long Stay automatically; you may override.
4. **Commercial** — choose the **rate basis first** (per room per night, monthly package or total package); every rate on the enquiry uses that basis. Enter the client's requested rate and your initial proposed rate. Variance, discount, room nights, nightly equivalent, potential revenue and expected value are calculated live. If a discount exceeds the company threshold the form warns you that saving will raise an approval request.
5. **Lead status** — defaults to New Enquiry. Closing as **Lost** requires a reason.
6. **Follow-up** — next follow-up date and next action keep the enquiry on your calendar.
7. Press **Save Enquiry**. Success is confirmed only after the record is stored centrally. If a similar enquiry exists (same company and property, arrival within two weeks) a warning lists it so you can avoid duplicates.

## Working an enquiry (detail page)
- **Log follow-up** — method, what was discussed, client response, next action, next follow-up date, optionally a new status. Each follow-up is a permanent timeline entry.
- **Quotation** — records the quoted rate (first quote becomes the proposed rate, later quotes the revised rate), reference and validity; status moves to Quotation Sent. Attach the quotation PDF under **Documents**.
- **Note** — internal note visible to management.
- **Edit** — change any field. Rate changes are kept in **Rate history** with your reason. If someone else saved the record while you were editing, you are asked to reload (nothing is overwritten silently).
- **Request rate approval** — when you need a rate below your authority. The enquiry moves to *Awaiting Internal Approval* until a manager or the commercial head decides; you are notified of the outcome and the approved rate is locked into the record.
- **Documents** — PDF, Word, Excel, PNG, JPEG or TXT up to 5 MB.

## My Enquiries
Searchable table of your enquiries with filters for month, stay month, property, status, segment, source, stay type and duration, rate band, follow-up due and date ranges. Overdue follow-ups are highlighted.

## Follow-up Calendar
Month view of scheduled follow-ups with an overdue list. Click an entry to open the enquiry.

## My Performance
Conversion, win rate, revenue, monthly trend, funnel and property split for your records.

## Notifications
The bell shows new lead assignments, approval outcomes, follow-ups due/overdue, inactivity and confirmed/lost alerts. E-mails are sent as well if enabled for your account.

## Sales managers
Managers additionally see the management navigation (Executive Dashboard, All Enquiries, Salesperson and Property Performance, Revenue Pipeline, Negotiation Tracker, Follow-up Monitoring, Reports) limited to their **direct team and assigned properties**. They can **reassign** enquiries within their team, **approve or reject** rate requests up to their authority limit (requests above it are routed to the commercial head) and **export CSV** of their scope.
